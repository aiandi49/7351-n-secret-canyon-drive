Ridgetop Homesite Overview

Set within The Canyons — the pinnacle of Catalina Foothills living — this 1.49-acre ridgetop homesite is one of the last true premier land offerings in Tucson's most exclusive guard-gated community.

Elevated and gently sloping, it offers a highly buildable envelope — a rare advantage in a mountainside setting where many remaining lots are defined by steep terrain and expensive site work.

The natural pad easily supports a single-level luxury residence of approximately 6,000 sq ft, allowing you to invest in architecture and finish rather than heavy excavation.

No heavy excavation required. The natural grade works in your favor — a rare advantage in the Catalina Foothills where buildable terrain commands a significant premium.
